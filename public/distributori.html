Which one are you?

Three situations, three different problems. Pick yours.

Your exclusivity is worth what somebody else lets it be worth

You paid for the exclusive rights to your country. Open your national marketplace and count how many sellers are offering the same product today. Nobody is enforcing what you bought.

We govern the marketplaces on the brand's behalf and have unauthorised sellers removed. The exclusivity you already paid for starts meaning something again.

And the second thing nobody says out loud: to hold the mandate you have to take container loads of new references without knowing which will move, tie up capital for months, and discount whatever is left to make room for the next shipment.

With us the brand's stock is already in Europe, cleared and inspected. You order three hundred units of the reference that is selling in Germany, you get them in 48-72 hours, and if it moves you reorder next week. If it doesn't, there is no container to clear out. We do not take the brand away from you — we take away the warehouse and the risk.

Between you and the manufacturer there are two or three margins

You buy from an importer, who bought from someone else. Every step takes its cut, and none of them was ever explained to you.

Here you buy at the price the brand set, not at the end of a chain. The commission is paid by the brand — never by you.

The goods are already inside Europe, already cleared, already inspected on arrival: 48-72 hours from Turin, correct VAT for your country, no container minimums.

And your territory stops being open ground: the brand decides who sells in your country, exclusively or shared among a few. Nothing is sold to anyone the brand has not approved.

The category your customers ask for, and you don't have

Arab fragrance is the fastest-growing category in European perfumery. Most established distributors watched it from a distance, because the supply chain was the problem: importing from the Gulf, customs, EU cosmetic compliance, containers.

That complication is gone. The Gulf houses keep their stock in our hub in Turin: already imported, already cleared, already compliant with EU cosmetics regulation, with us as their Responsible Person.

You buy at the brand's price, order what you need with no container minimums, and receive in 48-72 hours with the invoice already correct for your country.

In practice: you can open the category tomorrow, without importing anything and without risking stock. And you get in before the distributor down the road does.
