The question you will not put in an email

If a European structure grows your brand, will that structure one day dictate terms to you? Will you become a hostage of your own European channel?

It is the right question, and nobody asks it out loud. So here is the answer, in the terms that are written in our agreement — published in full, in seven languages including Arabic, before any negotiation:

  • The goods remain your property the entire time they are in our hub. We never buy your stock, so we can never hold it against you.
  • You set every price, country by country. We do not mark up and we do not discount.
  • You approve every distributor and you can revoke access instantly.
  • We are paid a commission on processed orders only. No fixed fee, no minimum, no shelf rental. If you sell nothing, we earn nothing.
  • We never compete with you. We do not own a competing brand and we do not sell to the final consumer.
  • You can leave. The terms of withdrawal are written in the agreement, public, before you sign anything.

We are not a distributor and we are not asking you to give anything up. We are the operations department you would otherwise have to open in Europe: a company, a warehouse, staff, a regulatory officer. Without opening it.
